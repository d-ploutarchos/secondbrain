# Awesome Decomp Games list

Points to Awesome Decomp Games, a list tracking 2,118 fan projects that rebuilt classic games to run natively on modern PCs, such as Ocarina of Time at 60 fps and Mario 64 with 16-player online co-op. Most need files from a copy you own.

**Why it matters:** Hobby/gaming curiosity; low lasting value.

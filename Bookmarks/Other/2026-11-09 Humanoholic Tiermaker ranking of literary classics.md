# Tiermaker ranking of literary classics

A Tiermaker chart of "the best classics of all time." Life-changing (SSS): Crime and Punishment, The Brothers Karamazov, Meditations. Essential (SS): War and Peace, Anna Karenina, The Iliad, The Odyssey. Below that: a deeply-impactful row (The Count of Monte Cristo, Les Misérables, The Divine Comedy, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, The Republic), then must-read modern and existential picks (1984, Brave New World, Camus, Kafka, Siddhartha, One Hundred Years of Solitude), down through the usual English-canon novels and a niche row (Ulysses, In Search of Lost Time, Walden, The Prince, The Art of War, The Aeneid).

**Why it matters:** A quick visual map of a popular classics canon, useful if you want a next serious book.

**What to try:** Pick one unread title from SSS or SS — Meditations or The Brothers Karamazov if you want the life-changing tier.

# High-DR profile pages as an SEO trick

Oleksii (@acecreamu) calls this a ridiculously simple SEO trick almost nobody uses: get a page or profile on high domain-rating sites and use those properties. He lists Chrome Web Store (DR 99), GitHub Pages (96), Medium and Stripe Climate (94), about.me, Crunchbase, Behance, G2/Capterra and Dribbble (91–92), Product Hunt, Gumroad, Linktree and Dev.to (90), plus Carrd, Hashnode, Substack, Taplink, mssg.me, TrustMRR, indiepa.ge, and Viralmarketinglab.

**Why it matters:** A concrete list of high-authority places where a profile or hosted page can create a backlink, complementary to yesterday’s “black hole SEO” original-research tactic.

**What to try:** Pick two or three sites you do not already use (GitHub Pages, Product Hunt, or Crunchbase are easy starts) and publish a profile or page that points at a property you care about.

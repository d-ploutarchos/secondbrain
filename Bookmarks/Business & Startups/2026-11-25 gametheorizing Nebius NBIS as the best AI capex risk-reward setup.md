# Nebius (NBIS) as the best AI capex risk-reward setup

Jordi Alexander (@gametheorizing) rarely shares a specific trade, but says he is heavily convicted on a risk/reward setup in Nebius ($NBIS) after months of hunting for the best way to invest in the AI trade across public and private opportunities. He thinks private high-yield AI deals will eventually be worth sourcing and possibly bringing onchain, but with the recent price dislocation he sees the greatest R/R on the public side in $NBIS. At the time of the post the stock was at $76-77; anything under $80 with a first target around $120 feels like one of the best +50% setups he has seen that can be done in size in a very long time. It had been trading at $160 only a couple of weeks earlier, hit by the market's unease about circular AI capex funding plus a delay at one Nebius site.

Why he picked Nebius, in short: an extremely robust balance sheet and conservative fiscal approach that should help it survive a liquidity airgap; high technical ability delivering and operating capacity, making it a preferred partner of NVIDIA, Databricks and others; an asset-light model (someone else funds the buildout) already generating large returns but underpriced because it is not marked as ARR; and other valuable balance-sheet assets, including a 25% stake in ClickHouse, that add more ways for things to go well. He frames this R/R as sitting at the top of "AI Capex Mountain" -- if the floodwater reaches that high, the entire AI sector is cooked anyway. A 50%+ bounce happening quickly would likely need some kind of liquidity injection; he does not think that is far off, but things might get worse first, so he would not use leverage. Within six months he expects the stock to trade a lot higher and is positioning accordingly. Not financial advice; he notes most people are already deployed and not sitting in cash.

**Why it matters:** A rare, detailed public-markets thesis from Jordi on how to express the AI capex trade after a sharp dislocation, with a concrete name, levels, and a framework for judging neoclouds (balance sheet, operating quality, asset-light revenue, hidden assets).

**What to try:** If you are looking at AI infrastructure exposure, read the post and check whether Nebius at these levels still fits the R/R he describes -- especially the asset-light model and the ClickHouse stake -- and treat it as an unlevered idea, not a trade to size with leverage.

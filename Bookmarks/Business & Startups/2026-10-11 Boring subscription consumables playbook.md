Advises selling boring consumables (dog supplements, refills, coffee pods) on auto-subscription to women 45-65. Suggests 15-20 products across 3-5 niches at $2-5K/month each, so no single product failure hurts much.

**Why it matters:** A portfolio-of-small-businesses idea, similar in spirit to your venture building.

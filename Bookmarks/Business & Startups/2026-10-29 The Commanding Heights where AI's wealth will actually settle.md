# The Commanding Heights: where AI's wealth will actually settle

Will Manidis and Joe Roberts publish a long essay a year in the making, arguing the benefits of abundant AI will accrue far outside San Francisco. Their thesis: like the printing press, railroads and the transistor, the inventors (the labs) won't capture most of the value, because model prices are collapsing (about 13x cheaper per year) while training costs rise about 3.5x a year. Value instead flows to whoever owns the 'rails' that carry intelligence into the wider economy: distribution networks, messaging apps, and dollar stablecoins, with the frontier itself increasingly controlled by the US and Chinese states. They also warn that AI capex, stock indexes and credit are becoming one correlated bet that Washington will feel forced to keep rising, and that AI will deepen the K-shaped economy. Very widely saved (12,000+ bookmarks).

**Why it matters:** A big-picture framework for where to invest and build in the AI era (own the distribution and payment rails, not the model), directly relevant to your ventures and your stablecoin and tokenization interests.

**What to try:** Read the full essay on commanding-heights.com and note which 'crossings' (distribution, payments, data) your own businesses could own.

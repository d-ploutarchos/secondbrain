# Agent swarms will target companies and large accounts

Jonny Miller (@jonnym1ller) warns that anyone running a $2M+ company, or with more than 100k followers on a platform, is likely to become a target in the coming weeks and months. He thinks founders are underestimating how agent swarms can be used to find vulnerabilities, leak private data, or run social-engineering and phishing campaigns. He says he recently used AtlasForgeAI, with permission, to red-team a friend's company and found serious exposure, and he is offering a paid white-hat review: $2k if they find a significant vulnerability, otherwise no charge.

**Why it matters:** A timely reminder that agent swarms change the threat model for ordinary companies and public accounts, not just high-profile targets.

**What to try:** Review how exposed your own products and accounts would be to automated probing, and decide whether an authorized security review is worth doing now.

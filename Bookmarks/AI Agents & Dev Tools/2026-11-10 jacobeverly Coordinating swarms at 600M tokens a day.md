# Coordinating swarms at 600M tokens a day

Jacob (@jacobeverly), quoting Matt Huang's "swarm riders" post, writes that two weeks of aiming at much larger outcomes and coordinating swarms to pursue them already feels like a 10x, if not 100x, jump in his personal impact. He is now using 8–16 agents and about 600 million tokens a day, aiming for 1 billion, with clear ROI that grows as usage grows. Boundless is rolling this out to the team. That scale, he says, makes taste, conviction, and ownership even more valuable: choosing worthwhile problems and defining verifiable success that creates business value. An under-discussed skill is keeping up without burning out. High-impact tools and frameworks arrive fast, and models keep absorbing their capabilities; adopting them at a fast but sustainable pace is how you keep building beyond what the models already do out of the box, and stay on the widening right side of adoption.

**Why it matters:** A first-person operating report that complements Huang's swarm-rider frame with concrete scale (8–16 agents, ~600M tokens/day) plus the overlooked constraint: sustainable adoption without burnout, and the rising value of taste and ownership.

**What to try:** If you scale a swarm, pair the token spend with explicit, verifiable success criteria, and set a sustainable pace for adopting new tools so the work stays ahead of what models already do unassisted.

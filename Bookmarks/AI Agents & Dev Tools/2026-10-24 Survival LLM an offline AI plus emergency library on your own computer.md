# Survival LLM: an offline AI plus emergency library on your own computer

Brangus (@RatOrthodox) built Survival LLM, a free, open-source app that runs a local AI model together with an offline library of about 1.5 million articles (medical references, FEMA and CDC guides, military survival manuals, iFixit, wikiHow and about 930,000 Wikipedia articles) with no internet needed. It picks a model to fit your machine's memory (from IBM Granite 8B on 16 GB up to Nemotron Super on 128 GB Macs), cites the source passage for every answer, and can also search your own PDFs and notes. Widely saved (8,000+ bookmarks).

**Why it matters:** A practical, private offline fallback for blackouts or travel, and a neat example of local-model retrieval done well with sources shown.

**What to try:** Try it: install Survival LLM on your Mac while online (about 9 GB library plus the model), add your own key manuals, then test it with Wi-Fi off.

App and source: https://www.brangus.io/survival-llm

# Turn the Fable 5.2 prompt guide into a skill

derrick (@dvsch) replies to Ben Kaufman, who found Fable 5.2 weaker on intent and proactivity than Opus 5.5 and said it needs longer, highly specified prompts. derrick’s workaround: the point of Fable is large tasks, so you need a clear, large prompt. Turn Anthropic’s Fable 5.2 prompt guide into a skill, feed that skill your big task, and iterate until the prompt covers everything — he likes /grill me for that pass — then run the big prompt.

**Why it matters:** A practical way to meet Fable 5.2’s appetite for long, specified prompts without rewriting a giant brief by hand each time.

**What to try:** Convert the Fable 5.2 prompt guide into a skill, then use /grill me (or the equivalent) to expand one real large task before you run it.

# Agentic Engineering in Grok Bot

Kun Chen (@kunchenguid) posted a live-session video, [Agentic Engineering in Grok Bot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_cVWs9pPFDI), walking through his real Grok Bot setup for maintaining open-source projects. In the video he says the setup helped him process over 2,000 issues and PRs in a month. He also shares three Grok bots: [Firstmate](https://x.ai/bot/__4FfrkUdvpdMk6-LKg5r) ("the only agent you talk to," orchestrating the rest), [Kun](https://x.ai/bot/xK8W0ukRv4iZjglzz-FRE) (a bot daily-synced with his own knowledge), and [Distill anyone](https://x.ai/bot/id4s2QYrPYZsiTqvzIhkt) (turn someone's public presence into a bot, optionally mirrored to a GitHub skill repo).

**Why it matters:** A practical, current walkthrough of a high-volume Grok Bot workflow from someone who already ships a lot of agent tooling, plus three bots you can actually try.

**What to try:** Watch the video, then try Firstmate as an orchestrator, and Distill anyone if you want a daily-updated bot (or skill repo) of a person.

# An AI-native macOS dock with focus modes

@trq212 shows a personal project: an AI-native macOS dock with custom modes (writing, building, etc.) to help stay focused. Their agent can change how each mode behaves and add new modes, and it classifies their activity and notifications in real time and acts on them. Shown in a short demo video; widely saved (5,000+ bookmarks).

**Why it matters:** A neat example of an agent that manages your computer environment and attention, not just tasks; an idea worth stealing for your own focus setup.

**What to try:** Watch the demo, then try a simple version: define two or three focus modes (e.g. writing, building) and have an agent mute notifications and set up the right apps for each.

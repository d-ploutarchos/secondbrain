# FOMO as fear of what missing out says about us

Argues FOMO often comes less from missing an opportunity than from what missing out would mean about us. Uses the example of seeing a peer launch a business, where desire quickly mixes with shame and urgency. Suggests noticing what FOMO asks you to believe before acting.

**Why it matters:** Clear reframe for reactive comparison and decision anxiety.

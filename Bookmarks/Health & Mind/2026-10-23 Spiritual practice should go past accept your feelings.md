# Spiritual practice should go past 'accept your feelings'

One-line take from @ftlsid: people doing spiritual practice often stop at feeling and accepting their feelings, and rarely go on to dismantle the illusions that produce the bad feelings so those feelings stop arising at all.

**Why it matters:** A useful prompt to notice whether your own inner work stops at acceptance or reaches the beliefs behind the feelings.

**What to try:** Next time a strong negative feeling comes up, after acknowledging it, write down the belief or assumption that triggered it and question whether it's true.

# Bryan Johnson's three levels of sauna

Bryan Johnson describes three dry-sauna levels. Level 1 is 11 minutes at 80°C (176°F), a starting point where heart rate rises and blood vessels widen; frequent sauna use is linked to lower cardiovascular and overall mortality. Level 2 is 20 minutes at 93°C (200°F); he cites studies linking 4-7 sessions a week to lower mortality, heart disease and dementia risk, and claims personal results like a 10+ year drop in vascular age and much less microplastics in his blood. Level 3 targets a core temperature of about 39°C (102.2°F), which took him about 31-33 minutes at 93°C (measured with a swallowed pill), to trigger heat shock proteins that help repair damaged proteins.

**Why it matters:** A simple, graded sauna protocol you can come back to; the personal results are his own claims, but the level 1-2 guidance matches the general research on regular sauna use.

**What to try:** If you have sauna access, start at level 1 (about 11 minutes at 80°C) a few times a week and build toward level 2.

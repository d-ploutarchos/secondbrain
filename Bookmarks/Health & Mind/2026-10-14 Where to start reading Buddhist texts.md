# Where to start reading Buddhist texts

Recommends starter anthologies for the Pali Canon: Bhikkhu Bodhi's "In the Buddha's Words" and Thanissaro Bhikkhu's "Wings to Awakening". For Mahayana, suggests the Diamond, Lotus and Heart Sutras among others.

**Why it matters:** A ready reading list if you go deeper into meditation.

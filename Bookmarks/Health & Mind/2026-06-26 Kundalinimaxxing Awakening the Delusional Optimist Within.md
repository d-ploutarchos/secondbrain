# Kundalinimaxxing: Awakening the Delusional Optimist Within

Long-form X article by VP (@VibesPatrol) that reads kundalini through C.G. Jung: the rising energy is a map of individuation, where the lower centres confront shadow and instinct, the heart opens to inner figures (anima/animus), and the higher centres integrate undeveloped functions. Chasing the physical symptoms alone (heat, visions, emotional storms) is framed as a path to inflation or destabilisation. It lays out a daily 45 to 55 minute protocol: set an intention and slow the breath, then spinal breathing with a light root lock, alternate nostril breathing, gentle spinal movement through the centres, optional Breath of Fire, and grounding; then shadow journaling, active imagination with an inner figure, and one small embodied action for the day. Widely saved (1,200+ bookmarks).

**Why it matters:** A concrete, structured routine that pairs breathwork with Jungian shadow work, worth coming back to if you want a daily inner-work practice rather than just reading about it.

**What to try:** Try a shortened version for a week: 5 min spinal breathing, 5 min alternate nostril breathing, 5 min grounding, then 6 min shadow journaling on 'what triggered me today and where is that in me?', and pick one small action. Skip Breath of Fire until the rest feels steady.

# Representations of other minds live in your own

ftlsid writes that 95% of human activity is an attempt to control how other people perceive you, or a proxy for that — but the representations of other people's minds live inside your own mind, so the whole struggle is the mind wrestling with itself.

**Why it matters:** A compact reminder that social self-consciousness is often an internal loop, useful when awareness work meets worry about how you come across.

**What to try:** Next time you catch yourself managing someone else's view of you, notice that the imagined audience is happening in your own mind, and stay with that rather than tightening around it.

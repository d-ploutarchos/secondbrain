# Ari Juels: from proof-of-work to Chainlink

Fan post about Ari Juels: he worked on e-cash before 2008 and coined the term 'proof of work', wasn't especially interested in Bitcoin, got hooked when a colleague showed him smart contracts in 2015, then co-wrote the Chainlink whitepaper and became Chief Scientist at Chainlink Labs. Ends with $LINK and a YouTube link.

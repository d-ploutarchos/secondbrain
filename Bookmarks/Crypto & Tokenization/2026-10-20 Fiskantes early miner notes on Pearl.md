# Fiskantes: early miner notes on Pearl

An early Pearl miner's eight-point take: he likes the proof-of-useful-work idea, but mining lost to buying OTC, liquidity is thin and sketchy (a Coinbase listing may come), the valuation is high for something this early, many insiders already hold bags, and issuance is steep like early ZEC. He sees maybe 2-4x upside on the AI x crypto narrative, compared with TAO, but not the lopsided trade crypto Twitter pitches.

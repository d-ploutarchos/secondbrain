# Long-time LINK bull sells 75% of his Chainlink tokens

@ClairHawk_Cap, a prominent Chainlink bull, says he sold 75% of his LINK tokens (average cost $18, taking a loss) to diversify, keeping exposure through $GLNK shares and June calls. His reason: his conviction in Chainlink's business hasn't changed, but Chainlink's success doesn't guarantee value accrues to the LINK token the way it would to a shareholder, tokenomics questions remain, and LINK trades with the wider crypto market. He's reallocating to stocks ($AMD, $HIMS, $NAUT as a high-risk pre-revenue bet, and $NFLX around 18x earnings) and keeping 10-15% cash.

**Why it matters:** A useful sentiment signal and a clear statement of the 'great tech, unclear token value capture' risk for anyone holding LINK.

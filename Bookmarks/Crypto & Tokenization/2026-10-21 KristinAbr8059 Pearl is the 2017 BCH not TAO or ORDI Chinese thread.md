# Pearl is the 2017 BCH, not TAO or ORDI (Chinese thread)

Chinese-language post after PRL doubled again. Argues Pearl is best compared with Bitcoin Cash in 2017: both are "better Bitcoin" chains built on Bitcoin code (Pearl forks btcd) that change proof of work to pull miners in, with Pearl swapping SHA-256 for matrix-multiplication work on GPUs. BCH briefly reached about 15-25% of BTC's market cap; Pearl is under 0.1% today, and the author's minimum success bar is 1-5% of BTC's market cap. The author holds a position and says so.

**Why it matters:** A useful historical frame for sizing Pearl's upside and failure mode, though it is a holder's view.

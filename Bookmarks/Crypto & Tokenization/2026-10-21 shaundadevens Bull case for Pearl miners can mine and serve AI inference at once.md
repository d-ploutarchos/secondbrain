# Bull case for Pearl: miners can mine and serve AI inference at once

Points out that 44% of public Bitcoin miners' megawatts are contracted to AI/HPC for 2026-28, with mining costs near $75k and the 2028 halving coming. Pearl's FP8 proof of useful work uses the same matrix math as AI inference, so miners can do both. Three bull cases: hashrate is the hardest valuation metric for store-of-value coins, Pearl removes the security bottleneck that held back alternative stores of value (it also targets quantum resistance, like ZEC), and miners who mine it become its advocates. Notes it is about 1/30th of ZEC's market cap and calls it ultimately a memecoin with a strong narrative.

**Why it matters:** The clearest bull counterpoint to the Fiskantes bear notes from yesterday; the numbers date quickly.

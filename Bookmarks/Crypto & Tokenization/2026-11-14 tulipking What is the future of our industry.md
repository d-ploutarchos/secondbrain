# What is the future of our industry?

Tulip King (@tulipking) shares a roughly 22-minute video on the future of crypto. The chapter list argues that analysis is too grounded in current systems; there is no perfect political system, only the right one for the time; tech fearing Democrats is the wrong boogeyman; why China, and why Nick Fuentes, look ascendant; crypto is a change technology (including meme coins, and crypto as both laissez-faire capitalism and shared equity); Vitalik versus Toly; cracks as the American Republic closes; crypto's own flaws; and a wrap that the new split is top versus bottom, not left versus right — crypto as protest technology.

**Why it matters:** A compact thesis that crypto's role is protest and regime change, not just a market inside the current system — a frame worth holding beside more market-structure or tokenization views.

**What to try:** Watch the video with the chapter list in hand, and notice where you agree or disagree that crypto is primarily a protest technology.
